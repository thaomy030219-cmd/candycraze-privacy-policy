Privacy Policy for Sudoku: Mystic Odyssey

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sudoku: Mystic Odyssey (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the App.

Application name: Sudoku: Mystic Odyssey
Package name: com.quyetdx90.sudokumysticodyssey

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to be played without creating an account and does not directly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, phone number, postal address, or date of birth.

Game progress, settings, completed stages, star ratings, puzzle state, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on your device. The App also uses third-party advertising services that may automatically collect or process certain information.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers where available, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Such information may be used to provide and measure advertising, analytics, improve advertising services, and prevent fraud and abuse.

See the Google Privacy Policy for more information.

3. Rewarded Advertisements

The App may allow you to voluntarily watch rewarded advertisements in exchange for in-game benefits such as hints, puzzle assistance, or additional time. Watching rewarded advertisements is optional. Rewards are provided only after the advertising system confirms successful completion of the required advertisement.

4. Personalized and Non-Personalized Advertising

Depending on your location, device settings, applicable laws, and consent choices, Google AdMob may serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. Where legally required, the App may request consent before certain advertising technologies are used. Advertising identifiers may also be managed through your device settings.

5. Game Data

The App may store game progress, unlocked and completed stages, best times, star ratings, the current puzzle and its progress, language and game preferences, and music, ambient sound, and sound-effect settings locally on your device. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling it may remove locally stored progress.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

7. Children's Privacy

Sudoku: Mystic Odyssey is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Where additional legal requirements apply, advertising and data-processing configurations may be adjusted as required.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the App. Game progress is primarily stored locally on the user's device. Information transmitted by third-party services is subject to the security practices of those providers.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game information generally remains on your device until it is cleared or the App is uninstalled. Information processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's applicable policies and retention practices. The App does not currently require a user account, so there is no in-app user account to delete.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, legal requirements, or data practices. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are made.
