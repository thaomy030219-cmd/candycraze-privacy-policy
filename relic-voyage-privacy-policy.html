This Privacy Policy explains how RELIC VOYAGE (the “Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the mobile game. We aim to collect only the information needed to operate, improve, secure, and support the Game and its advertising features.

1. Information We May Collect

The Game may process gameplay and technical information such as game progress, level results, scores, stars, collected relics, booster and life state, settings, app version, device and operating-system information, diagnostic information, and crash or performance data. Where online leaderboard or synchronization features are enabled, the Game may also use an internal player identifier and the player name or avatar selected inside the Game.

The Game does not require a Google account or other social-network account merely to play the core campaign.

2. Advertising

RELIC VOYAGE uses Google AdMob to display advertising, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app interactions, and advertising interaction data in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. Choosing not to watch a rewarded advertisement does not prevent access to the core Match-3 campaign.

3. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required by law or platform policy, the Game may present a consent or privacy-options message before using certain advertising or measurement technologies. Available choices may vary by country or region. Users may also be able to reset or restrict advertising identifiers through their device settings.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and save game progress; provide game features; manage lives, boosters, relic collections and rewards; show and measure advertisements; diagnose crashes and technical problems; prevent abuse or manipulation of online features; maintain leaderboards where enabled; and improve game balance, performance and user experience.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate features of the Game, such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud or leaderboard infrastructure, where those services are enabled. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the Game, or in connection with a lawful business transfer.

6. Game Progress and Local Storage

Core game progress may be stored locally on the user's device. This can include completed levels, best scores, stars, Coins, lives, boosters, Hidden Relics, Great Relic restoration progress, settings, and related game state. Uninstalling the Game, clearing app data, replacing a device, or device failure may cause locally stored progress to be lost unless a synchronization or recovery feature is available.

7. Online Features and Leaderboards

If online leaderboard or synchronization features are enabled, limited game data may be transmitted to a server, such as an internal player ID, display name or avatar selection, progress, best scores, star totals, relic totals, restoration totals, and technical information needed for integrity and synchronization. The core campaign is designed so that ordinary gameplay can remain available without a continuous network connection.

8. Data Retention

Local game data remains on the device until it is deleted, overwritten, reset, or removed with the app, subject to device behavior. Data processed by third-party services is retained according to those services' policies and applicable law. If server-side game data is introduced or used, it will be retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain security, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

9. Children's Privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Advertising and consent behavior may be adjusted according to the age rating, target audience, region, and requirements of Google Play and applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us using the address below.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the Game to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Choices and Requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about processing. You may contact us to make a privacy request. We may need enough information to identify the relevant game record and verify the request before acting on it.
