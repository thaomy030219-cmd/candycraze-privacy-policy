Privacy Policy for StampCam

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StampCam ("the App") handles information when you use the application. StampCam is a camera application that allows users to capture photos and add customizable elements such as logos, date/time stamps, and custom text.

1. Information You Provide

StampCam may allow you to select a logo or image from your device and enter custom text to be displayed on photos. These items are used to provide the App's watermark features. The App may store your selected settings locally on your device so that they can be reused in future sessions.

2. Camera and Photos

StampCam requires access to your device's camera in order to capture photos. The App may also request access necessary to select images or logos and to save completed photos to your device. Photos and selected watermark content are processed for the purpose of providing the App's functionality.

3. Advertising

StampCam may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Advertising services may collect or process certain information, such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information, subject to applicable law, device settings, consent choices, and Google's policies.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

4. Data Storage

App preferences, such as watermark settings, may be stored locally on your device. Photos created with StampCam are saved to your device according to the App's save settings and Android's media storage system.

5. Location Information

StampCam does not require precise location information for its current core photo watermark features. If location-based features are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate and any required permissions or disclosures will be provided.

6. Children's Privacy

StampCam is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us using the email address below.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services such as Google AdMob. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their data practices may differ from those of StampCam.

8. Security
